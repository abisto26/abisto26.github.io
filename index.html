About

“Fun is how I fix any problem. That’s the way I live.”

Abisto

Abisto is a Moroccan rider who loves life, loves people and is always smiling. He grew up in Marrakech, and that’s where the 26 comes from: it’s the city’s plate number. He got his first motorcycle at 14 and has been riding ever since. He even rode the whole coast of Morocco, all the way down to Laâyoune.

Today he lives in Casablanca, where the traffic is heavy and people are stressed. So he started talking to the drivers next to him at red lights. One day, at a very long light, he asked a stranger to play rock paper scissors. Instead of the usual words, he shouted “TaTaHi!” The video went viral, and a new habit was born.

Since then, the red light has been his stage. He plays, asks strange questions and makes people laugh. Those questions became his games, because he believes fun is the best way to learn. People now recognise him everywhere, but he hasn’t changed. He does it because it makes him happy, and he’s coming to more cities soon.
